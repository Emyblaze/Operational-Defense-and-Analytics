# Mapping the Bare-Metal Perimeter

**Status:** Draft / Outline

## The Core Premise
Before any offensive or defensive action can be taken, you must establish total visibility of the network topology. This goes beyond standard IP sweeping; it requires identifying the physical hardware acting as the backbone of the network.

## Operational Workflow to Develop:
1. **Passive Reconnaissance:** Leveraging open-source intelligence (OSINT) and DNS records to map external infrastructure without alerting the target.
2. **Active Fingerprinting:** Utilizing tools like Nmap to determine operating systems, open ports, and potential hardware configurations of active nodes.
3. **Physical Perimeter Correlation:** Matching digital footprints to physical locations (e.g., identifying localized servers vs. cloud-hosted instances) to understand the physical attack surface.
